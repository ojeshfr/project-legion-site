Telemetry Policy

Active when focused.
Paused when backgrounded.

Optional sensor polling runs only while the Project Legion window is focused. Minimizing, hiding, switching away, or leaving a game active suspends optional background timers without issuing stale UI writes.

Foreground Gating
Home and Thermals poll at 1 s and Battery at 5 s only while the window is active.
On-Demand Peripherals
Mouse DPI and polling settings are read on demand only—never on a background timer.

Hardware Workaround

Recover glitched keyboard lighting on wake.

On some Legion laptops, waking or opening the lid leaves the keyboard backlight stuck or dim. Project Legion includes an opt-in toggle on the Keyboard page that automatically resets the lighting controller when the lid opens.

1. Backlight Off (5 s)
When a lid-open event fires, the backlight turns off for a 5-second settle window.
2. Restore Preset 1
After the 5-second countdown completes, Preset 1 is re-applied automatically.
Capabilities

Focused controls across system and peripherals.

Built in native .NET 9 and WPF without web renderers or hidden background services.

Power Modes

Switch Quiet, Balanced, Performance, or Custom mode with configurable fan points and PL1/PL2 power limits.

Thermals & Sensors

View CPU package, GPU temperatures, and fan RPM at a 1 s foreground cadence that pauses when unfocused.

Battery Charge Modes

Toggle Conservation (80% cap), Normal, or Rapid Charge and monitor battery telemetry every 5 s while focused.

Display & GPU Modes

Change internal panel refresh rate, Overdrive, HDR, and Hybrid-Auto MUX policy with on-demand dGPU inspection.

RGB & Spectrum Keyboard

Configure 4-Zone and Spectrum presets, brightness levels, and the opt-in lid-open Preset 1 backlight reset.

Mouse & Macros

Adjust supported mouse DPI stages and polling settings on demand without background hardware polling, plus macros.

Automation & Packages

Trigger power, display, or lighting actions on AC, battery, or game events, and manage optional packages cleanly.

Verified Updater

Verifies asset byte size and SHA-256 before staged handoff, preserves user settings, and retains a rollback backup.

01 · Verification

SHA-256 & Size Checked

Downloaded ZIP archives verify byte size and SHA-256 checksum while held open before extraction. Checksums do not establish a trusted publisher; pre-release builds remain unsigned.

02 · Staged Handoff

UpdateHost & Protected Staging

Explicit confirmation extracts into a fresh version directory, preserves %LOCALAPPDATA%\ProjectLegion, and leaves the previous version intact.

03 · Recovery

Automatic & Manual Rollback

An early exit after updating restarts the retained previous version, alongside explicit one-click rollback inside the Updater page.
